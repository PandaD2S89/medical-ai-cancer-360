# F*CK Cancer — Medical AI Cancer 360°
An open collaboration to build AI that supports the whole cancer patient
 
  My father has stage 4 cancer. Through his journey, I realized something troubling: everyone seems to be looking at a different piece of the patient, but rarely the whole picture.

The oncologist focuses on the cancer. Other specialists focus on their respective areas. But who's connecting everything — nutrition, sleep, medications, organ health, muscle recovery, and emerging research?

I started using AI to investigate those connections, including drug repurposing and new scientific discoveries. My father's cancer has been shrinking, and he's made it farther than we once feared possible. I can't say which factors contributed to his improvement, but the experience convinced me that there may be valuable opportunities we're overlooking.

What if we could give every cancer patient a better fighting chance?

I'm not a medical professional or an expert programmer. But I believe I have the intuition, curiosity, and determination to recognize opportunities, question assumptions, and bring together people who can make a difference.

That's why I started Medical AI Council 360°.
We've developed and individually tested eight specialized AI modules covering oncology, nutrition, rehabilitation, sleep, organ health, medication intelligence, and quality of life, coordinated through a central intelligence system.

Early integration tests have demonstrated communication between these specialized systems. Our next challenge is completing integration, strengthening safety and reliability, and validating the system.
We're new to the GitHub community, and this is our very first public introduction to the project.

We've been developing the concept and testing its individual components, but we're now taking our first step toward inviting others to participate.

We're here to learn, collaborate, exchange ideas, and welcome people who believe in the mission. We don't pretend to have all the answers, and we welcome constructive feedback from experienced developers and medical researchers.
Imagine an affordable AI companion on your phone that brings together specialized AI researchers, examines the whole picture, challenges its own findings, and helps families understand opportunities and concerns they can discuss with their doctors.

Not a replacement for medical professionals. Not a promise of a cure.

A powerful tool to help patients and families navigate cancer with more knowledge, better coordination, and greater support.
Developers, AI engineers, medical researchers, healthcare professionals, and anyone who has experienced the impact of cancer.

I can't build this alone. But together, we may be able to create something that could help thousands of families.

I believe this technology should be accessible to everyone, regardless of income or technical knowledge.

Cancer doesn't discriminate. Access to helpful information shouldn't either.

If you believe in this vision, tell us what skills you bring and how you'd like to contribute.

F*CK Cancer. Let's give families a better fighting chance.
